"Substantiated" counts only transactions whose proof document is actually inside this ZIP, under proof_of_transaction/ — it is the same number as the file count beside it and as "Proofs of Transaction" in README.txt. "Linked to transfer" is not a receipt: it means the row was matched to its counterpart transaction (the other side of an internal transfer), which documents that the money moved between your own accounts but is not a proof of a purchase or a sale. "No receipt needed" rows are self-evident (bank fees, interest, internal transfers) and require no supporting document — their amounts are still included in every total on this page.
